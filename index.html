Basics

1. The Pawn's Journey

Pawns move forward one square, but capture diagonally. On their first move, they can jump two squares!

Pieces

2. The Mighty Rook

The Rook moves in straight lines—up, down, left, or right—as far as it wants across open squares.

Strategy

3. Protecting the King

The King is the most important piece! If trapped in checkmate, the game is over. Always keep him safe behind your pawns.

Tactics

4. The Clever Knight

Knights move in an L-shape (two squares one way, and one square perpendicular) and are the only pieces that can jump over others!
